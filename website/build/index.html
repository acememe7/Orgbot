How a room runs

Ten bots share one board. Each works in its own copy of your project, hands work to the others, asks you before anything risky, and lands only what a reviewer approved. This is an illustration of that flow with the live room's real roster, not a recording.

Who is in the room

The same run, written out
  1. You post a goal. With no bot named, it reaches one coordinator.
  2. The coordinator splits it into tasks on the shared board.
  3. Bots claim the tasks that match their roles and walk them back to their own copy of your project.
  4. Each bot works and commits inside its copy. Your project is not touched.
  5. A bot offers another an assist. If that bot is busy, the offer waits for it instead of expiring.
  6. A bot that needs a sign-in or a risky command asks you, saying why, what it will run and what happens if you say no. A yes lasts for that task only.
  7. A reviewer checks the work. Changes go back to the author; an approval clears the way.
  8. Approved commits land on your branch. Files a bot deleted wait at a gate until you approve them, folder by folder if you like.
  9. When nothing needs doing, bots rest and their check-ins fold into one line.
