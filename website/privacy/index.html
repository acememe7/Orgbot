Privacy policy

This page describes the public OrgBots website and the OrgBots Linux desktop application.

About the website

The website is static and does not provide accounts, forms, analytics, advertising, or embedded third-party widgets. Its pages do not set optional tracking cookies. The site uses a small local browser preference to remember whether you dismissed the privacy notice; it is not sent to OrgBots.

The site is delivered through Cloudflare and our hosting provider. Cloudflare may use essential security cookies and network-error reporting. The website host may process connection details such as IP address, request path, and time to serve pages, protect the service, and diagnose errors. Origin access logs rotate daily with 14 retained rotations. Cloudflare processes security data under its own retention policies. Contact theaece@theaecestudio.com for questions about website data.

Information on your computer

OrgBots is a desktop application. Room history, project workspaces, usage records, and settings are stored locally as part of the application’s operation. The application does not send this data to an OrgBots website account or service.

Provider connections

When you ask a provider tool to work, the prompts, selected project content, attachments, and tool results needed for that work may be sent to the configured provider or MCP service. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. OrgBots does not control their retention or use.

On Linux, the workspace sandbox protects project writes but does not fully isolate readable host files, credentials, network access, or remote tools. Read the workspace safety guide and only connect tools and accounts you trust.

Backups and sensitive content

Portable backups are private, unencrypted files with a 64 MiB size cap. They can contain room and project information. Protect the backup file and its destination. Secrets pasted into agent history may remain in local records and should be treated as exposed to any provider that received them.

Your choices and requests

You choose which providers and tools to configure and what information to include in a task. You can remove local application files using your operating system, taking care to preserve anything you still need. Provider-side copies and retention are governed by the provider. To ask a question about this policy, email theaece@theaecestudio.com.

Changes

This policy may change as the product or website changes. The date at the top identifies the current published revision.

No optional cookies or analytics are used by this site. Hosting security logs may apply.
